A collecting game for your Discord server

Sift for 45 Siftlings, hunt numbered relics only one player can own, and battle and trade with friends. Your collection follows you to every server. Sift also brings moderation, protection and community tools, all switched off until you turn them on.

Collect

Shinies, relics, evolved forms, badges and a Siftdex to complete.

Community

Tickets, welcome messages, leveling, giveaways, birthdays, temp voice and optional server tag roles.
