Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what the Sift Discord bot ("Sift", "we", "us") stores, why, and what you can do about it. We only store what Sift needs to work. We never sell your data, and Sift has no ads or trackers.

What we store

DataWhy
Your Discord user IDTo link your game progress, levels, reminders and other features to you.
Game progress: shells, Siftlings, relics, gems, crates, badges, streaks, battle results, trades, market listings and bidsTo run the Siftlings game. This is shared across all servers.
Server-specific stats: levels and XP, message and activity counts, server tag wearing, server economy balancesFor leveling, activity rewards, server tag roles and leaderboards.
Things you choose to give Sift: your birthday (day and month only), reminder text, marriage partnerSo those features work. You can remove them at any time.
Moderation records: warnings and cases with the user ID, moderator ID, reason and timeSo a server's staff can keep a moderation history.
Server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and settings chosen by server staffTo remember how each server has set Sift up.
Server backups made by staff: role, channel and permission layoutSo staff can restore their server's structure. Backups don't contain messages.
IDs of tickets, giveaways, starboard posts and temporary voice channelsTo keep those features working.

Messages

Sift reads messages in servers that use it to give XP and activity rewards, count messages, run counting games and auto-responses, and detect scam links. Messages are checked as they arrive and are not saved by Sift. The exceptions are text you give Sift on purpose, such as a reminder, a warning reason or an auto-response set up by staff.

If a server turns on logging or tickets, Sift posts things like edited or deleted messages and ticket transcripts into that server's own channels. Sift does not keep a copy; that server's staff control those channels.

What we don't collect

No email addresses, passwords, IP addresses, real names, payment details or direct messages. We don't use cookies or analytics on this website.

Where it's stored and who can see it

  • Sift's data is stored in a database on a server rented from PebbleHost in Europe.
  • Daily backups of that database are kept for 7 days in case something goes wrong.
  • Only the Sift developer can access the database. Some data is visible to other people through Sift's features, for example leaderboards, profiles, and moderation cases shown to a server's staff.
  • Sift works through Discord, so Discord also processes this data under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • We don't share or sell data to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep data while it's needed for Sift to work, or until you ask us to delete it. Data removed from the database disappears from the backups within 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data Sift has about you. You can remove your birthday and reminders yourself with Sift's commands. Server owners can ask us to delete all data for their server. If you're in the EU or EEA, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet).

Deleting your data also deletes your game progress, and it can't be undone. Moderation records that a server's staff keep for safety may be kept for that server even after other data is deleted.

Children

Sift follows Discord's minimum age rules and is not meant for anyone below Discord's minimum age in their country.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

To ask about your data or to request deletion, contact us in the Sift support server, linked on Sift's profile in Discord.
